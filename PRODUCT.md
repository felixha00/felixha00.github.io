# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is a mix of potential employers and clients evaluating Felix Ha's design judgment, UI craft, technical range, and ability to turn ideas into polished interactive work. Visitors are likely scanning quickly at first, then diving into projects when the interface signals enough taste, rigor, and capability to earn attention.

## Product Purpose

This site is Felix Ha's personal portfolio. It exists to showcase multidisciplinary work across software, web, hardware, visual branding, and ventures while making the site itself a demonstration of design eye and interface skill. Success means the portfolio feels memorable, technically assured, and precise enough that visitors trust Felix with ambitious visual and product work.

## Brand Personality

Experimental, precise, technical. The brand should feel engineered and hands-on, with a restrained industrial confidence inspired by Teenage Engineering: tactile, gridded, functional, slightly strange, and deeply intentional. It should flex interface craft through motion, typography, interaction, and layout without becoming ornamental noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS portfolio patterns, over-polished agency templates, cute or pastel personal sites, stock "modern developer" aesthetics, and soft rounded card grids that make the work feel interchangeable. Avoid treating technical design as a costume: monospace, pixels, grids, and motion need to feel earned, not decorative.

## Design Principles

1. Make the interface part of the portfolio: every surface should demonstrate judgment, not merely contain work.
2. Let precision carry the experimentation: unusual details should be backed by alignment, hierarchy, accessibility, and responsive behavior.
3. Show range without losing authorship: software, hardware, branding, and ventures can vary, but the portfolio should still feel unmistakably Felix.
4. Favor tactile systems over templates: grids, rulers, pixel type, and mechanical interactions should feel like instruments, not effects.
5. Keep the path to evidence short: visitors should quickly understand what Felix made, why it matters, and where to inspect deeper work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, and semantic structure. Support reduced motion and make sure no meaning depends on color, animation, hover, or visual texture alone. Experimental visuals should enhance the work without blocking comprehension for assistive technology, low-vision users, motion-sensitive users, or people scanning on small screens.
